# Privacy Policy for SkyCover

**Last Updated:** October 24, 2026

**Perera Labs** ("we," "our," or "us") develops and operates the **SkyCover** mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our App.

By using SkyCover, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Location Data
* **Precise or Approximate Location:** To provide real-time cloud cover, sky conditions, and weather data relative to your surroundings, SkyCover may access your device's geolocation (GPS or network-based).
* **Usage Scope:** Location data is accessed **only in the foreground** while you are actively using the app or requesting forecast updates for your current area.
* **Storage:** We do **not** log, store, track, or share your historical location trails on external servers. Coordinates sent to third-party weather API providers are strictly used ephemerally to fetch local atmospheric and solar data.

### B. Device and Diagnostic Information
* SkyCover does not collect personal identifiers such as your name, phone number, physical address, or contact lists.
* Standard non-personally identifiable diagnostic data (such as OS version, device model, or crash stack traces) may be processed locally or via standard Google Play services to ensure app stability.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services

SkyCover relies on third-party service providers to deliver weather information and core platform functionality. These services have their own privacy policies governing data handling:

* **Google Play Services:** Used for app delivery, license checks, and system APIs.  
  [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* **Weather & Atmospheric APIs:** Coordinates (latitude and longitude) are transmitted securely over HTTPS to weather data providers solely to query cloud cover, solar, and forecast data.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties or data brokers.

---

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion

* **Ephemeral Processing:** Location coordinates and weather queries are processed in memory and are discarded once the forecast is loaded or cached locally on your device.
* **Local Storage:** Any saved favorite spots or settings are stored locally on your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy

SkyCover is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.

---

## 5. Security

We take the security of your data seriously. All network communications fetching weather and sky conditions utilize secure Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS) encryption.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
